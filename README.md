# Olist E-Commerce: Hyper-Personalized Marketing Analytics

##  Business Problem
Mass marketing in e-commerce leads to inefficient ad spend and low engagement. Olist needed a data-driven approach to discover cross-sell opportunities and accurately target high-value customers.

##  Key Analytical Methods & Findings
1. **Directional Product Bundling Analysis:** 
   - Initial Apriori Market Basket Analysis revealed >90% single-item transactions.
   - Shifted focus to directional cross-sell probabilities, discovering a **10.96% conversion opportunity** when offering Linen/Sheets to Home Comfort buyers (vs. 0.46% in reverse).
2. **RFM Segmentation:** 
   - Segmented the entire customer base into 6 distinct tiers.
   - Identified **33,373 high-value users** (27,053 Loyal Customers & 6,320 Champions) driving platform revenue.
3. **Precision Target Audience Mapping:** 
   - Filtered exact marketing targets for cross-sell campaigns (e.g., 929 Champions & 3,143 Loyal Customers in Home Comfort).

##  Impact & ROI
Transformed raw transaction logs into an actionable, targeted marketing list maximizing campaign ROI by focusing promotional budgets exclusively on high-conversion customer segments.

##  Dataset & Citation
- Dataset: [Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist (Kaggle)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce)
